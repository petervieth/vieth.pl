Team Play Hockey

Five-on-five where one skater cannot beat three. Puck control drains in traffic, goalies get beaten by puck movement, and your teammates play a real system: wall support, a net-front body, D on the points.

Systems

Coaching aids

Players

Press any button on a controller to make it show up. Pick a position for each player. Put two players on the same team to play as linemates. Drills always put players on Home. Leave everyone off to watch the AI teams play.

Controls

Move left stick or D-pad · WASD / arrows
Pass A · J or Space (aim with the stick)
Shoot X or B · K (press early for a one-timer)
Chip / dump Y · L (goes where the stick points)
Without the puck Pass calls for it (or switches skater with lock off). Shoot pokes from a stick length, or lifts his stick when you're on him
Pause Start · Esc
Game speed View/Back · − and = keys
Quiz move your marker, Pass to lock it in (mouse drag works too)
